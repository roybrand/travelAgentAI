AI Layer
I'd use LangGraph, not plain LangChain.
Why?
Your app has state.
For example:
User says
"Actually remove the rental car."
The AI should understand that this means:
hotel changes
routes change
restaurants nearby change
attraction timings change
LangGraph excels at this because it models workflows as a graph rather than a single prompt.

One feature I'd add that could make this stand out
Most travel apps stop after booking. Your AI could become a live travel companion.
For example, while the user is travelling, it could automatically notice:
a flight delay and rearrange the day's itinerary,
heavy rain and replace outdoor attractions with indoor ones,
a restaurant cancellation and suggest a similar nearby option,
cheaper tickets becoming available,
local events happening tonight,
unexpected traffic affecting planned routes.
Instead of being a planning app, it would continuously optimise the trip from booking until the traveller returns home. That kind of end-to-end intelligence is still uncommon and could be a strong differentiator.
